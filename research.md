 Research — Assignment Reminder

 1. The Problem (5 Lines)

    1. Professors share assignments with students through Google Classroom.
    2. Sometimes, professors give only one day to complete an assignment.
    3. My friend received the assignment notification but decided to complete it later.
    4. He got busy with other work and forgot to submit the assignment.
    5. I want to build a tool that reminds students about their pending assignments before the deadline.

2. Existing Solutions

1. Google Classroom Notifications
- Google Classroom sends notifications when professors share assignments.
- My friend received the notification but still forgot to complete the assignment.
- So, additional reminders may help him remember his pending work.

 2. Google Calendar
- Google Calendar allows users to add assignments and set reminders.
- But students have to add every assignment manually.
- This extra work may not suit my friend because assignments are already available on Google Classroom.

 3. Sources I Read

Source 1: Google Classroom Notifications

Link: https://support.google.com/edu/classroom/answer/6141557

- Google Classroom provides notifications about classroom activities.
- Students can manage their notification settings.
- These notifications help students stay updated about assignments.

Source 2: Google Calendar Tasks

Link: https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/9901136

- Google Calendar allows users to create tasks.
- Users can add due dates and reminders.
- Students can use these features to track their assignment deadlines.
